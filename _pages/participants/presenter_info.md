## Presentation Material Submission

All presenting authors should submit their presentation materials via the [AACL-IJCNLP 2026 presentation submission portal](https://aaclijcnlp-2026-presentations.paperform.co/):

- Pre-recorded presentation video
- Poster PDF
- Presentation slides

**Main conference and Findings papers**: all three materials (video, poster PDF, and slides) are required.

**Workshop papers**: requirements are decided by each workshop's organizers — please follow the instructions from your own workshop. The video, poster, and slides fields are optional in the portal for workshop papers.

If you cannot find your paper in the submission portal, please contact your workshop organizers or the conference program chairs so that the paper list can be updated.

Further presenter instructions will be posted on this page as they become available.
