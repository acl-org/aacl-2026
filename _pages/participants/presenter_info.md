## Presentation Material Submission

All presenting authors should submit their presentation materials via the [AACL-IJCNLP 2026 presentation submission portal](https://aaclijcnlp-2026-presentations.paperform.co/) by **November 2, 2026**. For step-by-step instructions, please read the **[Presentation Recording & Submission Guide](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1nAezbzgckj3WKgncHUN0pqZW-jNv7yOg_EiBU8-_ia4/edit)**.

- Pre-recorded presentation video
- Poster PDF
- Presentation slides

**Video length requirements**:

| Track | Pre-recorded Video |
|:---|:---|
| Main conference long papers | 10 minutes (required) |
| Main conference short papers | 5 minutes (required) |
| Findings long papers | 10 minutes (required) |
| Findings short papers | 5 minutes (required) |
| Demo papers | 5 minutes (required) |
| SRW oral papers | 10 minutes (required) |
| SRW posters and workshop papers | Optional (10 minutes) |

Poster PDF and slides are required for main conference, Findings, demo, and SRW papers. Workshop requirements are decided by each workshop's organizers — please follow the instructions from your own workshop.

Please name your files with the conference and paper ID so they can be easily identified (e.g., `AACL 2026_main-291`, `AACL 2026_Find-456`, `AACL 2026_Demo-99`). Maximum file size: 20 MB (PDF, PPT, PPTX).

For detailed recording instructions (environment setup, audio, and recording tools such as PowerPoint and ScreenPal), see the [Presentation Recording & Submission Guide](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1nAezbzgckj3WKgncHUN0pqZW-jNv7yOg_EiBU8-_ia4/edit).

If you cannot find your paper in the submission portal, please contact your workshop organizers or the conference program chairs so that the paper list can be updated.
