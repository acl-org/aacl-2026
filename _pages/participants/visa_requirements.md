## Visa Information

Visitors to the mainland of the People's Republic of China must obtain a visa from one of the Chinese diplomatic missions unless they are citizens of one of the visa-exempt countries.

## Visa Exemption

Holders of ordinary passports issued by the following visa-exempt countries may enter China without a visa as long as their visit does not last longer than the visa-free period listed below, unless they are allowed to extend their stay.

![Visa-exempt countries and visa-free periods for entering mainland China](/assets/images/china-visa-exemption-countries.png)

For the latest visa exemption policy, please refer to the [Visa Exemption Policy by Chinese Visa Application Service Center](https://www.visaforchina.cn/MAN3_EN/tongzhigonggao/187900100801073152.html).

## Visa Application

A visa is required for all overseas participants, except those from visa-exemption countries, to attend the conference ONSITE.

Chinese visa processing may take a few weeks. As a result, participants are strongly encouraged to apply as early as possible ahead of their departure date (at least **TWO months** is suggested) in order to allow sufficient time for their application to be processed and, if approved, subsequent visa issuance in their passport.

- [China Online Visa Application (COVA)](https://consular.mfa.gov.cn/VISA/)
- [Chinese Visa Application Service Center](https://www.visaforchina.cn/VIE3_EN/qianzhengyewu)
