## Visa Information

Visitors to the mainland of the People's Republic of China must obtain a visa from one of the Chinese diplomatic missions unless they are citizens of one of the visa-exempt countries.

There are several visa options for attendees. We strongly recommend that you first explore visa-free entry, transit visas, or a tourist (L) visa before considering an F visa. If you have further questions, please consult your nearest Chinese embassy or consulate for the most up-to-date guidance.

### 1. Visa-Free Entry

Holders of ordinary passports from eligible countries may enter China without a visa for short-term visits.

![Visa-exempt countries and visa-free periods for entering mainland China](/assets/images/china-visa-exemption-countries.png)

For the latest visa exemption policy, please refer to the [Visa Exemption Policy by Chinese Visa Application Service Center](https://www.visaforchina.cn/MAN3_EN/tongzhigonggao/187900100801073152.html).

### 2. Transit Visa

[Citizens of ~55 countries](https://en.nia.gov.cn/n147418/n147463/c183412/content.html) can transit through China visa-free when traveling onward to a third country:

- 24-hour visa-free transit (with interline ticket)
- 72-hour / 144-hour visa-free transit (54 eligible countries)
- 240-hour visa-free transit

**Note:** Usually not suitable for conference attendance unless you are genuinely in transit.

### 3. L Visa (Tourist Visa)

Intended for tourism, sightseeing, or personal visits.

- Required for attendees from countries not on the visa-free lists
- Typically valid for 30–90 days (single or multiple entry)
- Requires: valid passport, proof of accommodation, return/onward flight ticket

### 4. F Visa (Visit/Exchange Visa)

Intended for non-commercial exchanges, academic visits, and short-term professional events.

- Recommended for invited speakers, presenters, and academic participants
- Requires an official invitation letter from the AACL-IJCNLP 2026 Organizer (see [Invitation Letters](/participants/invitation_letters/))
- Usually valid for 30–90 days

### Quick Reference Guide

- **If your country is on the 30-day visa-free list:** You may not need a visa.
- **If your country is on the 10-day transit list only:** You likely still need a visa for AACL-IJCNLP 2026.
- **If you are an invited speaker/presenter:** Request an F Visa invitation letter from the organizers.
- **If you are attending as a general participant or tourist:** Apply for an L Visa.

### Important Note for Attendees

⚠ Visa policies can change. Always confirm the latest requirements with your local Chinese embassy/consulate before applying.

Chinese visa processing may take a few weeks. Participants are strongly encouraged to apply at least **TWO months** ahead of their departure date to allow sufficient time for processing and visa issuance.

Visa application portals:

- [China Online Visa Application (COVA)](https://consular.mfa.gov.cn/VISA/)
- [Chinese Visa Application Service Center](https://www.visaforchina.cn/VIE3_EN/qianzhengyewu)

For information about invitation letters, please see the [Invitation Letters](/participants/invitation_letters/) page.
