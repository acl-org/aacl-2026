A regular invitation letter will be issued automatically upon completion of the registration. If you don't need the letter urgently, please wait until registration opens.

## Expedited Invitation Letter

For those who need to expedite the process, please provide all of the following information and documents in your request and send it to [aacl2026@iccmacao.com](mailto:aacl2026@iccmacao.com).

**Required information for all applicants:**

- Your full name as shown in your passport (as it will appear on the letter)
- Date of birth and nationality
- Your passport number (if you wish to include it in the invitation letter)
- Your title and affiliation (department and university or company)
- Affiliation address
- Official contact email address

**If you are an author:** Please include the title of your accepted paper at AACL-IJCNLP 2026.

**If you are not an author, please include:**

- An up-to-date Curriculum Vitae (CV) including a list of publications (if applicable)
- A brief statement explaining your interests in the conference
- A list of any recent AACL / EMNLP / ACL conferences you have attended, and/or a reference in the NLP community who has attended past AACL/ACL/EMNLP conferences

## Privacy Statement

All personal information received will be treated as strictly confidential. The information will only be used for the purpose of issuing the requested invitation letter.
